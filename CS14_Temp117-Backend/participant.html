Eye-Tracking Calibration Guide

Before we start, please read through these steps to ensure accurate data collection.

  • Sitting distance — Sit about 50–60 cm from your screen, roughly arm's length away.
  • Lighting — Make sure your face is evenly lit. Avoid bright lights behind you or strong shadows.
  • Head position — Keep your head still and centred. Resting your chin on your hand can help.
  • Follow the dots — A blue dot will appear at 16 positions around the screen. Move only your eyes (not your head) to look directly at each dot.
  • Stay relaxed — Blink normally. The system collects data automatically once it detects stable gaze.
  • Takes ~30 s — The calibration runs automatically after you grant camera access.
